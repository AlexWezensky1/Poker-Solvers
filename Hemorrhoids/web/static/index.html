  • Equity is the share of the pot a hand takes on average, counted over every runout. It's the only figure here that is a share of the pot rather than a frequency, and the hands always add up to 100%.
  • High is the share of the high half a hand wins, counted only over the runouts that reach a high/low showdown. A pot won outright is never split into halves, so those runouts contribute nothing here.
  • Low is the same figure for the low half. One hand can take both halves at once, because it carries a separate low and high total, which is how a lone ace scoops a lone jack.
  • Scoop is how often a hand takes the whole pot, whether by emptying out alone before the last community card, by being the only one left holding all five, or by winning both halves at showdown.
  • Out is how often a hand empties out, on any street. Going out before the last community card wins the pot outright, so an early out is worth far more than a late one.
  • Keep is how often a hand still holds all five cards once the last community card is turned. A lone keeper takes the whole pot.
